From inputs to meaning

A worked example to check

With the illustrative inputs, 10 grams at SAR 500 gives SAR 5,000 of metal. A making charge of SAR 20 per gram adds SAR 200, producing a SAR 5,200 subtotal. The entered levy is zero, so the total stays SAR 5,200 and the final cost is SAR 520 per gram. These figures describe this example only.

02Use and understanding

Understand the rate before comparing offers.

Gram price, purity and making charges describe different parts of an offer. Keep the units and price basis consistent as you work through the example.

01 /

Match weight, karat and price

Use the gold alloy weight after excluding stones and separate non-gold components. Match its karat to the price field: a quote for one gram of 21K already reflects that karat. Multiply it directly by the alloy weight. Applying 21/24 again would count the purity adjustment twice and understate the metal value.

02 /

Put making charges on one basis

This calculator charges workmanship per gram. If your bill instead lists one fixed making charge, divide that amount by the gold alloy weight before entering it here. Check whether the quoted gram price already includes workmanship. Recording the same charge in both fields would increase the total twice.

When checking the metal line of a bill, the gold rate page provides karat tables alongside making-charge information. Match the table unit to your invoice, then return to the separate charge fields here. Keep the reference time with your notes so that a changing metal quote does not look like a change in workmanship.

03 /

Treat the levy as a scenario

The entered percentage applies to the combined metal and making-charge subtotal. It does not determine a legally applicable tax rate or taxable base. Use it to inspect a stated billing scenario. A zero entry means the model adds nothing; it does not establish that a transaction qualifies for an exemption.

A useful answer

Questions that help you use the guide

Are stones valued here?

No. Their weight is excluded from the gold calculation, and their separate price is outside this tool.

Does the page supply a current quote?

The displayed inputs are examples. Replace them with a documented quote and note its date, currency and karat.

Does the total predict a resale payment?

No. A later buyer may use different metal references, deductions and fees. This calculation describes the bill you enter.

Method and references

The rule and the limits of the tool.

Equations convert the values you enter. The guide does not assay gold or provide an investment valuation. Karat divided by 24 is a nominal purity calculation. Actual tax treatment depends on the supply and invoice, not on a calculator field.
